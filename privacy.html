Ownwise Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Ownwise: Warranty Tracker is built so your information stays on your iPhone. The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Items, receipts, photos, warranty details, notes and reminders are stored only on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to any server. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to scan or add a receipt or warranty card. Text is read on your device using Apple's Vision framework and, where available, Apple Intelligence on-device models. Images are never sent anywhere.

Face ID

If you turn on app lock, iOS handles Face ID or your passcode. Ownwise never sees or stores biometric data.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No notification data leaves your device.

Purchases

Ownwise Pro is a one-time In-App Purchase processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details. Your purchase is verified on your device using Apple's StoreKit.

Exports

Claim Pack PDFs and CSV files are created on your device and shared only when you choose where to send them.

Deleting your data

Delete any item inside the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

Ownwise does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
